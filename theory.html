SUPER-RESOLUTION MICROSCOPY / FIELD GUIDE

Beyond a bigger image.

Magnification enlarges a picture. Resolution determines whether nearby structures can be distinguished. Super-resolution methods change how spatial information is encoded or measured.

Why conventional fluorescence imaging loses detail

A point emitter forms a diffraction-broadened point-spread function (PSF). Nearby emitters produce overlapping intensity patterns. For an ideal circular pupil, the Rayleigh separation is 0.61λ/NA; it is one convention, not a universal boundary. Noise, background, sampling and aberrations also affect distinguishability.

SIM · known patterns reveal hidden spatial information

Illumination and sample structure multiply, producing Moiré information within the detection bandwidth. Phase steps separate the mixed contributions; orientations supply information along different directions. Pattern calibration and specimen stability matter.

STED · narrow the region allowed to fluoresce

A doughnut-shaped depletion beam drives excited fluorophores back down by stimulated emission. Fluorescence remains near the central intensity zero. The ideal width scales as d₀/√(1 + I/Iₛₐₜ), under idealized saturation and beam-shape assumptions; real performance depends on photophysics and alignment.

Open STED workbench · Explore depletion and PSF narrowing →

STORM · localize sparse flashes over time

An isolated broad spot can have its center estimated much more precisely than its width. Too many simultaneous emitters create overlap and biased fits. Repeated sparse frames build a localization map; labelling density, drift and fitting quality still limit structural resolution.

Research: which limitation comes next?

4Pi-SIMFLUX combines lateral structured excitation with axial interferometric information. LA-SIM connects selective activation to structured readout and computation. Electrochemical Multicolour STED separates labels through distinct electrochemical fluorescence responses.

Keep precision, resolution and label separation distinct

Localization precision describes uncertainty in an estimated position. Spatial resolution describes separable structure. Optical sectioning rejects out-of-focus contributions but alone does not surpass diffraction. Multiplexing distinguishes labels and does not automatically sharpen a PSF.

Scientific sources & model assumptions

Every lab identifies its synthetic data, simplifications and numerical procedure. The models are educational illustrations, not validated instrument predictions. Published performance is not a guaranteed simulator result.
