Presentation and remaining gaps

Poster titles are HTML, not embedded in the image files. Backgrounds belong to the page design. Instrument photography is not a simulated image; the numerical laboratories separately label synthetic results.

The collection includes three instrument photographs, nine specimen micrographs/composite figures, a published MINFLUX setup/data figure and one historical portrait. No verified photographs of the specific three frontier instruments are included. The SIM and MINFLUX figures have separately recorded open licences. Frontier posters use explicitly labelled contextual micrographs; these are not presented as results from the featured frontier paper. Round 4B-1 reuses the fluorescence and pollen micrographs and adds the TEM micrograph to the Optical/Electron lessons. Their equipment photographs also use the existing local assets.
